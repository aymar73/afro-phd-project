**The Afro Project** is a PhD research initiative at McGill University that designs a statistically validated, algorithmically enforced digital settlement currency for intra-African trade. It combines cointegration analysis, structural VAR, multi-agent reinforcement learning (MARL), and Kolmogorov-Arnold Networks (KAN) to build a monetary architecture that preserves national sovereignty while enabling continental integration.

## Research Question

> *Can a cointegration-based digital settlement currency with algorithmic enforcement reduce transaction costs in intra-African trade while preserving monetary sovereignty?*

The project addresses the **$14.4 billion annual leakage** from currency fragmentation in African trade (42 currencies for 54 countries, ~8% average transaction costs). It proposes a **three-zone architecture** (West, East, Southern Africa) instead of a single continental currency, based on statistical evidence that cross-zone cointegration does not exist.

## Key Findings

### Preliminary Results (20 years of data, 54 countries)

| Zone | Member Countries | Cointegrated Pairs | Status |
|------|------------------|--------------------|--------|
| **West** | Nigeria, Ghana, Senegal, Ivory Coast | 6/6 (100%) | Eligible |
| **East** | Kenya, Tanzania, Rwanda, Uganda, Ethiopia | 10/10 (100%) | Eligible |
| **South** | South Africa, Botswana, Namibia | 3/3 (100%) | Eligible |
| **Cross-Zone** | All cross-regional pairs | 2/45 (4%) | Not Eligible |

### Systemic Safety (VaR Stress Test)

| Metric | Value |
|--------|-------|
| Total EAC Banking Assets | $88B |
| Max Afro Exposure | $440M |
| 99% VaR (1-in-100) | $4.4M |
| **Max Loss as % of Assets** | **< 0.6%** |

**Conclusion:** Even in total failure, the Afro cannot trigger a systemic banking crisis.

## Methodology

The project combines four methodological pillars:

| Pillar | Technique | Purpose |
|--------|-----------|---------|
| **1. Cointegration** | Johansen Trace Test | Identify statistically viable currency zones |
| **2. Structural VAR / VECM** | Narrative sign restrictions | Analyze monetary transmission and deviations from equilibrium |
| **3. Multi-Agent RL + KAN** | Explainable AI governance | One KAN-based agent per central bank, with cooperative learning |
| **4. Algorithmic Enforcement** | Hyperledger Fabric smart contracts | Automatic, non-political ECM penalties |

### The Discovery Engine

A key innovation is the use of **Kolmogorov-Arnold Networks (KAN)** as a *discovery engine* — not just a forecasting tool. KAN can learn the functional form of economic relationships directly from data, and its learned splines can be extracted as symbolic formulas. This allows the system to discover **novel, zone-specific economic laws** that standard econometrics cannot detect.
